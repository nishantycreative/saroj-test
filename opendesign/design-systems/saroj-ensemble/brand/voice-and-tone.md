# Voice & Tone — Saroj Ensemble

## Positioning line
"Bold luxury for the modern Indian woman." Heritage craft, contemporary mind.

## Voice
- Confident, editorial, sales-aware — a premium house that knows how to sell without shouting.
- Sentence case in body copy; all-caps only for tracked labels and display headlines.
- Personal ("you", "your drape") but with a firmer commercial edge — "limited drop", "only X left" urgency where real, never fabricated panic.

## Tone by surface
- Editorial (hero, story, journal, lookbook): measured, evocative — "Where silk remembers the loom."
- Commerce (products, rails, deal strip): decisive and value-forward — discounted price + MRP + % OFF, star ratings, urgency tags ("Only 3 left"), countdown-driven sale framing.
- Utility (FAQ, forms, drawers): plain, generous, reassuring — never cold.

## Words we use / avoid
- Use: drape, weave, handloom, zari, heritage, atelier, ensemble, occasion, craft, limited, edit, drop
- Avoid: cheap, exclamation-mark spam, gimmicky pressure — urgency must feel real (countdown strip, limited stock tags)
- Numbers: Indian formatting ₹1,24,500; "₹" before amount; metres for fabric ("m"), price per metre ("₹/m")

## Punctuation & formatting
- No emoji in copy. Exclamation marks only in the promo strip (a genuine sale moment).
- Prices: `₹ 24,500` with thin space; sizes `S / M / L / XL`; dates `Mon, 12 Oct`.
- Buttons: "Shop the Collection", "Book a Styling Session", "Explore the Journal" — verb-first, two to four words.
